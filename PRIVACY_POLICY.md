# Privacy Policy — Masjid Timings

**Effective:** 2026-04-25
**Operator:** Konic IT (eesa.co.in)
**Contact:** mdaneesahmed@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what data the **Masjid Timings** app
(`in.co.eesa.masjid_mobile`) collects from you, why, and what we do with it.

## 1. Data we collect

### 1a. Without sign-in (browse-as-guest)
- **Device location** (GPS) — used **only on-device** to sort masjids by
  distance and display the "outside coverage" banner. Never sent to our
  server unless you tap "Use my current location" in the admin masjid edit
  form (only available to admins). No background tracking.
- **Anonymous request metadata** — IP address and request timestamps in
  standard server logs, retained for 30 days for abuse prevention.

### 1b. When you sign in
- **Name, phone number, email** — provided by you at sign-up, or fetched
  from your Google account if you sign in with Google.
- **Google profile picture URL** — when signing in with Google.
- **Roles & memberships** — which masjids you are a member or admin of.
- **Favourites** — which masjids you have starred.
- **Time-change suggestions** you submit.

### 1c. When you opt in to notifications
- Notification preferences are stored **only on your device** in
  SharedPreferences. Notifications are scheduled locally on the phone — no
  push tokens are sent to any server.

## 2. How we use your data

- To display masjid information, prayer timings, and sort masjids by
  distance from your location.
- To let admins manage masjid information.
- For one-time emails or in-app messages related to your account or
  membership requests (e.g. approval notifications). We do not send
  marketing communications.

## 3. What we do NOT do

- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for
  advertising.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** display third-party advertising in the app.
- We do **not** transmit your live location in the background.

## 4. Third-party services we rely on

- **AlAdhan API** — for sunset (Maghrib) calculation per masjid coordinates.
  Only the masjid's lat/lng is sent (not your personal data).
- **Google Sign-In** — when you choose to sign in with Google. Subject to
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **OpenStreetMap (Nominatim/Overpass)** — used during initial masjid
  data import. No personal data is sent.
- **Hetzner** — our backend server is hosted in Germany on Hetzner.
  Standard server logs (IP, timestamp) are retained briefly.

## 5. Your rights

- **View your data** — sign in and visit Profile → see your details and
  memberships.
- **Delete your account** — email mdaneesahmed@gmail.com requesting account
  deletion. We will remove your user record, favourites, and any
  suggestions/memberships within 7 days.
- **Revoke Google access** — at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## 6. Data retention

- Account data is retained until you request deletion.
- Server logs are retained for 30 days then automatically rotated.
- Cached data on your device is cleared when you uninstall the app.

## 7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from
children under 13 without parental consent.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the "Effective" date
and post a notice in the app's Announcements feed.

## 9. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, email **mdaneesahmed@gmail.com**.
